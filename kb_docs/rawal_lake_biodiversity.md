# Rawal Lake Biodiversity

Rawal Lake, an artificial reservoir in Islamabad, serves as a crucial wetland ecosystem and a haven for diverse aquatic and terrestrial species.

## Aquatic Life
The lake supports various fish species and aquatic plants:
- Common Carp (Cyprinus carpio)
- Grass Carp (Ctenopharyngodon idella)
- Water Hyacinth (Eichhornia crassipes)
- Various native aquatic plants

## Bird Life
Rawal Lake is a significant bird habitat, especially during migration seasons:

### Resident Birds
- Little Bittern (Ixobrychus minutus)
- Black Kite (Milvus migrans)
- House Crow (Corvus splendens)
- House Sparrow (Passer domesticus)

### Migratory Birds
- Various waterfowl during winter
- Wading birds
- Raptors

## Surrounding Vegetation
The lake's periphery features:
- Eucalyptus plantations
- Acacia species
- Native shrubs and grasses
- Ornamental plants in nearby parks

## Conservation Importance
- Important bird area (IBA)
- Wetland ecosystem services
- Recreational value
- Water supply for Islamabad

## Best Observation Spots
1. Dam area - Good for waterfowl
2. Reed beds - Bittern and other marsh birds
3. Surrounding parks - Urban wildlife
4. Viewpoints - Panoramic bird watching
