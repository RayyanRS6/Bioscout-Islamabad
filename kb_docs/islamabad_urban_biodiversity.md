# Islamabad Urban Biodiversity

Islamabad's urban areas feature a unique blend of planned green spaces and natural habitats, creating diverse micro-ecosystems within the city.

## Urban Flora
Common urban trees and plants include:
- Gulmohar (Delonix regia)
- Bougainvillea (Bougainvillea spectabilis)
- Jacaranda (Jacaranda mimosifolia)
- Eucalyptus species
- Various ornamental plants

## Urban Wildlife
The city supports diverse wildlife adapted to urban environments:

### Birds
- House Crow (Corvus splendens)
- House Sparrow (Passer domesticus)
- Rose-ringed Parakeet (Psittacula krameri)
- Black Kite (Milvus migrans)
- Various garden birds

### Mammals
- Rhesus Macaque (Macaca mulatta)
- Small rodents
- Bats

### Insects
- Butterflies
- Bees
- Various pollinators

## Green Spaces
Key biodiversity hotspots in urban areas:
1. Shakarparian Park
2. Rose and Jasmine Garden
3. F-9 Park
4. Various sector parks and green belts

## Conservation Challenges
- Urban development pressure
- Habitat fragmentation
- Invasive species
- Pollution
- Human-wildlife conflict

## Citizen Science Opportunities
- Bird watching in parks
- Butterfly monitoring
- Tree mapping
- Wildlife corridor documentation
